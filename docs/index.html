Why did GEMSDOE32's h33-h33-2-b2 score 0.2778 — and can it be beaten?

Score class: 0.2778 is OWNER-REPORT (project ledger; no organizer receipt in any inspected repo — IR-73-02). Everything below about the file's construction is MEASURED-HERE from its bytes (sha256 c55bafc470…), re-verified this session.

  1. Exact lineage: H33-2-B2 (37,654 dots) ⊂ D2.8 (44,090) ⊂ H19-5 (121,131), zero exceptions. H33-2-B2 is D2.8 with every dot within 2.0 px of the catalogue deleted — 6,436 dots (minimum retained distance 2.236 px). (This site previously printed “1,152 dots from ~38,800” — wrong, corrected; IR-73-11.)
  2. Dotting raises credit density: DTI = T / (0.2·(T+F) + 0.8·|G|). A non-redundant dot helps only if its kernel credit k > 0.2·DTI ≈ 0.056 at DTI 0.2778. Thinning 121,131 px to ~2.5–2.8 px-spaced dots keeps trace coverage (k ≥ ~0.58 on a covered trace) while paying the 0.2 FP tax once per ~6 px of trace: OWNER-REPORT ladder 0.1922 → 0.2477/0.2600.
  3. The B=2 prune is free: hidden truth excludes the catalogue, so dots ≤200 m from known traces mostly cover masked, zero-credit traces at full FP cost. Deleting 6,436 of them cost no hidden recall: 0.2600 → 0.2778 family step.
  4. The field matched the hidden set's likely construction: H19-5 is ~90% scarp/openness-weighted — the same LiDAR-scarp population expert re-mapping adds. Corollary (MEASURED-HERE): the champion scores DTI 0.0067 against the full catalogue, below random — any non-fold-fair catalogue holdout ranks it last (IR-73-06).

Can 0.2778 be beaten? 0.4413? At fixed mass the only lever is mean credit per dot; emission tricks are exhausted (ladder gains +0.0555, +0.0123, +0.0070). Beating 0.2778 needs a better field; 0.4413 (live #1, ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED public leaderboard read 2026-10-21) needs ≈+60% credit density over H33 — not claimed here. H73-7's measured claim is narrower and real: on the shared fold-fair instrument its field beats the champion-analogue control by +46% relative. Whether that transfers to the expert hidden set is the open question one slot would answer.
