1 Hydrothermal Permeability Corridor — THIS FILE (GEMSDOE73)
Dilatational jog × alteration × thermal coincidence		 dilatation, sis_rate, mapped trace set (for tip distance), rtp/tmi, GeoDAWN radiometric K/Th/TC, thermal inertia proxy (det_elev slope as albedo surrogate), 3DEP LiDAR scarps Product H = D0.6·A0.4 of (i) dilatation jog proxy: fault-trace curvature × tip-crack distance (Coulomb 25° extensional jog, σ-filtered) and (ii) alteration-thermal proxy: K enrichment (Th/K residual) + mag-low halo + thermal edge; Poisson-2.8 greedy emission on the product Blind geothermal faults under Quaternary cover have no scarp/lineament, so pure-structure detectors miss them; but they leak fluids → alteration (illite→K) + magnetite destruction → mag low + thermal high. Requiring the coincidence of permeability and alteration selects exactly the geothermal-active subset the metric rewards, suppressing barren bedrock faults that dominate the catalogue and its holdout proxy. New object: 35 channels in GEMSDOE32 have per-band ridges and pixel-wise products (H32-A/B/C); H33-A has orientation order parameter; none has a permeability × alteration product where one factor is derived from fault-tip mechanics and the other from hydrothermal geochemistry. Prior H32/H33 surfaces are all structure-only. Low — all layers are in training_features.tif + GeoDAWN rad + 1 m LiDAR links (competition CSV, official). No new external data. ~40 s CPU IMPLEMENTED & SHIPPED

2 Multi-scale curvature worm persistence
Topographic scarps that persist across scales		 det_elev, det_elev_slope, 3DEP 1 m DEM (official links CSV) Scale-space Frangi/Sato ridge response at σ=1.2, 2.5, 5.0 px on −det_elev; per-pixel worm score = minimum over scales × orientation coherence over 80 m window; ridge NMS → Poisson-2.8 Post-Quaternary scarps that survive erosion are visible at multiple scales; single-scale curvature also fires on fluvial terraces and road cuts that fool the catalogue. Persistence across scales selects tectonic scarps, which the catalogue under-maps under sagebrush/alluvium. Extend: GEMSDOE32 uses single-scale curvature/ridge; multi-scale persistence is not in 72-feature stack. Low — official DEM only RANKED — not shipped — held for next weekly slot if H73-E needs data
3 Long-straight conductive basement step
Buried basin-margin faults		 cond_surf, depth_to_base_surf, iso_grav_anom Straight gradient ridge: large gradient magnitude and orientation coherence >1 km window; product of normalized |∇cond|/|∇depth| and structure-tensor coherence; long-line vote as in H33-B/C Basin-bounding faults under 100s of meters of fill are invisible at the surface but sharp in conductivity/basement depth; the given catalogue is dominantly surface-mapped. Long straight steps are basin edges, not short alluvial noise. Overlaps H33-D in concept but here implemented on the 1 m LiDAR composite + cond depth, not cond alone; novelty is the long-window coherence gate (km, not px). Low RANKED
4 Aligned drainage-valley chains (H33-B revisit) det_elev, det_elev_slope Valley skeleton (NMS ridge of −det_elev) × 9-px 16-direction collinearity vote, de-emphasised on steep slopes Faults with no preserved scarp still deflect and align drainages; a single straight valley is a stream, a chain of collinear valley segments across drainages is a structure. Catches the same blind-fault class as #1 but via geomorphology rather than geochemistry. Already implemented in GEMSDOE32 H33-B — measured there as below random on catalogue-hidden (0.018–0.024 vs random 0.0344) and only winner on SGMC off-catalogue (0.0907 vs 0.0606). Instruments disagree in sign → not re-shipped alone. Low MEASURED — not promoted
