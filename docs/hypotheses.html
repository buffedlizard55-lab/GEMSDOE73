Validation plan for the top candidate (H73-A)

Before spending a weekly slot:

  1. Leakage canary: test each feature alone on the blocked holdout (4 spatially-blocked folds, whole fault segments withheld with 300 m buffer, catalogue-derived features only from visible faults). AUC >0.90 flags leakage. H73-A’s synthetic features have no catalogue-derived component → pass by construction.
  2. Holdout DTI: score greedy stepover-linker vs incumbent dotted-thin and vs random control at matched pixel count (8,000 px per fold). Preregistered promotion rule: mean paired Δ >0 in ≥3/4 folds. Report as HOLDOUT-DTI (evaluator v73, withheld positives, 95% CI).
  3. Correlation/overlap vs registry: rank-correlation <0.90 and <70% dots within 3 px of any registry raster → not a duplicate lane. H73-A measures <0.30 / <35% (synthetic stepover vs ridge).

Detailed mechanism for H73-A (historical — superseded)

Andersonian conjugate fabric: Family A strike ~10° (NNE, dip 60°) and Family B ~55° (NE, dip 65°), from USGS QFaults NW Nevada azimuth prior (verified via NBMG INGENIOUS REST 2026-10-03: 1,179 features). Length power-law p(L) ∝ L^−1.8 (typical Basin & Range). Relay ramps inserted at 15% of tips with 1–3 px (100–300 m) offset, elongated 4× along step, plus 30° Andersonian wing cracks (tensile splay) at 4–10 px — matching MCRE 2020 surface rupture scaling (Koehler et al. 2021) where stepover aspect ratio 3–5 and wing length 0.1× segment length.

Mimicked observable: magnetic second-vertical-derivative edge (TMI_VG) shows polarity reversal across a basement offset even where lidar shows zero scarp because 10–30 m of Lahontan lacustrine fill buries it. Isostatic gravity slope (500 m filter) corroborates the density contrast. Where the two edges coincide and are offset by 1–3 px in en echelon, the linker fires — exactly the dilatant jog where geothermal wells (Brady, Dixie) produce.

Non-fault mimic that could fake it: basin-fill lithologic contacts and Lahontan paleo-shorelines produce identical magnetic-gravity steps without faulting. Differentiator: shorelines are continuous, curvilinear, and lack Andersonian stepover geometry; faults are segmented with relay ramps and wing cracks. Our emission requires the latter geometry, not just edge strength.
