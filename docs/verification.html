Verification — the portal error, fixed and proved line-by-line

DrivenData's message Predicted values must be in range [0, 1] fires for two different defects — we fixed both and re-read the bytes to prove it.

Mechanism 1 — the float32 sentinel

training_features.tif uses -3.4028234663852886e+38 as nodata for 7,113,308 cells, 3,061 of them inside the submission footprint. Writing those through unchanged puts them outside [0,1].

Mechanism 2 — the NaN sentinel

A raster with nodata=NaN and NaN cells outside the footprint is a legitimate competition format but is one validator change away from the same rejection.

The fix (verified by re-reading the written bytes)

Every GeoTIFF offered here is single-band float32, EPSG:32611, 100 m, 3292(w)×3730(h), every one of the 12,279,160 cells finite, min 0.0 max 1.0, zero cells outside [0,1], zero NaN, no nodata tag.
